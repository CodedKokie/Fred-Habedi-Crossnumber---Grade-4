# Fred-Habedi-Crossnumber---Grade-4
An interactive, browser-based **math crossnumber** for learners to practise arithmetic while solving a puzzle. No installation, sign-up, or internet connection needed after download — it's a single self-contained HTML file that runs in any modern web browser.

---

## What it does

Instead of letters, every square in the grid holds a **digit**. Instead of word definitions, every clue is a **calculation** (addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division). The learner works out the answer to each sum and types the digits into the matching squares on the grid.

The grid has 5 **Across** clues and 5 **Down** clues. Several answers cross each other, sharing a square — so an across answer and a down answer must both agree on the digit in that shared square, just like letters overlap in a normal crossword.

The app gives learners instant, private feedback:
- **Check answers** — marks every filled square green (correct) or red (needs another look), and reports a score.
- **Reveal a square** — gives a one-square hint when stuck.
- **Clear grid** — resets the puzzle to try again from scratch.

---

## How to play (the rules)

1. **Click any square** to start, or click a clue in the Across/Down list to jump straight to it — the whole word highlights so you know exactly which squares belong to it.
2. **Work out the sum** shown in the clue.
3. **Type one digit per square.** Typing automatically moves you to the next square in that word.
   - **Arrow keys** move left/right/up/down between squares.
   - **Backspace** clears a square and steps back.
   - **Click a square a second time** to toggle between its Across word and its Down word, if it belongs to both.
4. **Shared squares must match.** If a square is part of both an across answer and a down answer, only one digit can go there — so it has to be the correct digit for *both* sums at once. This is the built-in cross-check that makes the puzzle self-correcting.
5. Click **Check answers** at any time to see which squares are right so far. A square only needs to be filled to be graded — you don't have to finish the whole grid to check your progress.
6. The puzzle is solved when every square is marked correct.

---

## What learners gain

- **Mental arithmetic practice** across all four operations (+, −, ×, ÷), including two- and three-digit numbers and simple division facts.
- **Self-checking numeracy** — because crossing answers must share the same digit, learners get an automatic hint that a mistake exists even before pressing "Check," encouraging them to double-check their own working rather than relying only on the app.
- **Working-memory and planning skills** — solving a crossnumber means holding onto partially-solved answers, revisiting them as neighbouring clues get solved, and reasoning about which digit could fit both directions.
- **Low-stakes, private feedback** — mistakes only show as a colour on screen, with no penalty, so learners can retry and build confidence without fear of "getting it wrong" in front of others.
- **A sense of completion** — the win state ("Solved!") gives a clear, motivating goal to work towards, similar to a game rather than a worksheet.
